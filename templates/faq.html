Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tarot reading?

You can book a reading by clicking the "Book Now" button under each service on our Services page. This will direct you to the booking form, where you can complete your reservation.

What can I expect from a One Card Draw?

The One Card Draw is a focused reading designed to answer a specific question or provide guidance on a single issue. This quick reading is perfect for clarity on the go.

How long does a Three Card Draw session last?

The Three Card Draw generally lasts 15-20 minutes, during which we explore past, present, and future energies to gain insights into your question or situation.

What should I prepare for the Astrology Birth Chart Interpretation?

Please have your birth date, time, and location ready, as these details are essential for creating an accurate chart. This personalized reading offers deeper insight into your life path.

How is the Personalized Guided Meditation structured?

Our guided meditation session combines your birth details and numerology to create a tailored experience aimed at bringing peace and clarity to your mind. Each session is designed to match your unique needs.

What is your refund policy?

Refunds are available if cancellations are made 24 hours in advance. For same-day cancellations, the fee will be credited toward a future session instead.
