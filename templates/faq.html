Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tarot reading?

You can book a reading by clicking the "Book Now" button under each service on our Services page. This will direct you to the booking form, where you can complete your order.

What can I expect from a One Card Draw?

The One Card Draw is a focused reading designed to answer a specific question or provide guidance on a single issue. This quick reading is perfect for clarity on the go.

What does a Three Card Draw cover?

In a Three Card Draw, we focus on three distinct areas: past influences, present circumstances, and future possibilities. Each card symbolizes one of these timeframes, offering a view that clarifies your situation from several angles.

What should I prepare for the Astrology Birth Chart Interpretation?

For an accurate interpretation, please prepare your birth date, time, and location. These essentials allow us to construct your birth chart and reveal the astrological factors that shape your life path and character.

How is the Personalized Guided Meditation structured?

Our personalized guided meditation is crafted using your birth details and numerology to create a session that resonates deeply with your unique energy. Each meditation is thoughtfully designed to address your specific needs, promoting peace, clarity, and alignment within. We ensure a meaningful, customized experience for you in particular.

What is your refund policy?

We do not provide refunds. Each reading or guided meditation is crafted with care, drawing on ancient practices and deep insight to provide you with meaningful guidance. We ask that you request a service only when fully committed, as we devote time and focus to each one, ensuring it is carefully aligned for you.
